🚀 What Is chiaki-rust?

chiaki-rust is a complete rebuild of the popular chiaki-ng remote play application, now powered by Rust for uncompromising speed and reliability. It lets you play your PlayStation 5 or PlayStation 4 games on your Windows computer with a GPU-resident 60 fps video path — meaning your graphics card handles the heavy lifting for the smoothest possible experience.

No programming skills needed. Just download, install, and play your favorite PS titles using your DualSense or other controllers.

🖥️ System Requirements

Your PC should meet these minimum specs for the best experience:

  • OS: Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit)
  • CPU: Quad-core Intel Core i3 or AMD equivalent
  • GPU: Dedicated graphics card with 2GB+ VRAM (NVIDIA recommended for VSR)
  • RAM: 8GB minimum, 16GB recommended
  • Storage: 200MB free space
  • Network: Stable broadband internet (minimum 15 Mbps for 1080p, 25+ Mbps for 4K)

Note: Both your PC and PlayStation should be on the same network for best performance. You can also enable remote connection over the internet via your router settings.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

🔹 Do I need to install anything else?

No, chiaki-rust is a standalone application. All required components are bundled. If you plan to stream to OBS, just install OBS Studio separately (free).

🔹 Can I use a PS4 controller?

Yes, DualShock 4 and DualSense both work. You can also use generic XInput controllers (Xbox) for basic controls.

🔹 What if my game lags or stutters?

Check your network connection (use Ethernet if possible), reduce stream resolution in chiaki-rust settings, or lower in-game graphics settings on your PS5/PS4.

🔹 Is this software safe?

Yes, it's 100% open-source (AGPL-3.0). You can review the entire codebase on GitHub. It connects only to your PlayStation console and required services.
